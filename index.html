Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Bachelor Degree in Law
  • Recognized University Qualification
  • Bar Council Registration Mandatory
  • Citizen of India

Practice Experience

  • Minimum 5 to 10 years practice based on post
  • High Court practice mandatory
  • Experience certificates required
  • Professional standing verification required

Required Documents

  • Bar Council Certificate
  • Educational Certificates
  • Experience Certificates
  • Reported Judgements
  • Passport Size Photograph

Important Instructions

  • Incomplete applications will be rejected.
  • All uploaded documents must be self-attested.
  • False information will lead to rejection or termination.
  • Appointment will be purely on contractual basis.
  • Candidates must satisfy all eligibility conditions prescribed in the application rules.
